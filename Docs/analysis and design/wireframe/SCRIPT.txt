# Opening
Today, I will introduce our project, **DRL Tracker**.

DRL Tracker is a web platform that helps students find and register for activities, manage participation evidence, and track their conduct scores.

Currently, activity information is shared across different channels like Facebook, Clubs, Email..., making it difficult for students to follow schedules and deadlines. Our goal is to bring these functions together on one platform.

## Slide 1 – User Accounts and Roles

First, we have **User Accounts and Roles**. Our system has three main user groups: Students, Organizations, and Admins.s

Students can search for activities and track their conduct scores. Organizations can create and manage events. Admins can manage users and approve activities before publication.

We referred to the systems of University M and University H when planning these roles.

Now, I will start with the functions for student accounts.

## Slide 2 – Activity Search and Registration

Next is **Activity Search and Registration**.

Students can search for activities by criteria /crytiariần or faculty, view event details, and register for activities they are interested in.

We referred to University M's registration interface to plan this function.

## Slide 3 – Personal Activity Tracking

The next function is **Personal Activity Tracking and Evidence Management**.

Students can check the conduct criterion /crytiariần, manage their evidence, and submit complaints or additional documents when needed.

We referred to the criteria /crytiariần table from University M and the complaint function from University H.

## Slide 4 – Conduct Score Dashboard

Next is the **Conduct Score Dashboard**.

Students can view their conduct scores and identify criteria /crytiariần they still need to complete.

We plan to adapt the chart style from University M to show three types of scores: the maximum score, the estimated /áttơmaytịt score the student may achieve, and the score that has been officially confirmed.

## Slide 5 – AI Advisor and Notifications

We also propose two additional features.

First, **AI Advisor** recommends activities based on students' interests and progress, helps them identify missing criteria /crytiariần.

Second, **Notifications and Reminders** informs students about registration deadlines, upcoming activities, and important updates.

These features aim to help students manage their activities more effectively.

## Slide 6 – SUMMARIZE

Finally, this table summarizes the main functions of the systems at University M and University H, which we used as references /rátrphầnsịt for DRL Tracker. And our interview findings.

Based on these systems, we aim to combine activity registration, evidence management, and conduct score tracking, AI Advisor and Reminders in one platform.

Thank you for listening.
